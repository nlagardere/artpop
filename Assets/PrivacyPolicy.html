Active Information Collection.  When you launch the App, the App creates an identifier for you and asks you for your name.  We use this information, together with your geographic location (collected with your consent), a self-portrait or other image that you take with the camera on your mobile device (collected with your consent), and your answers to a series of questions the App asks you, to create an “Aura.”  You may bypass providing geolocation information and photographs during the Aura creation process.  

Additionally, you may register within the App for an account on the Service.  An account you create will be associated with your Aura, and is necessary for you to engage in certain activities on the Service, such as syncing certain User Content to the cloud in the Service and posting to the Service’s “ArtHaus Exhibition.”  To register for an account, you will need to provide Personal Information, such as your email address and a username and password that you select.  

We also collect User Content that you create within the Service.  User Content may include photographs or videos that you take with your device or that are in your device’s photo or video libraries.

Using the Service.  We collect information when you use the Service.  For example, we collect information about what tracks from the album ARTPOP (“Album”) may be resident on your device and whether you obtained those tracks via a download from an authorized digital distributor of the Album (e.g., Apple iTunes).  The App obtains this information by scanning the music library on your device when you launch the App.  If you have purchased tracks from the Album via Apple iTunes or Amazon, or have acquired a lawfully released physical copy of the Album on CD (as defined in the EULA) or via an otherwise authorized digital distributor, then you may be able to access those tracks in or through the App following compliance with the verification procedures set forth in the EULA.  As described in the EULA, the verification process may require you to provide us or our third-party service provider with information such as an email address and a user-selected password (as part of the CD Confirmation Procedure, as defined in the EULA), or with evidence of an Alternative Digital Purchase (as defined in the EULA), i.e., a copy of a digital receipt.

We also use Google Analytics to track activity within the App, including how many people visit the iTunes Store or other music stores via the “Buy” button in the App, and roughly how many people have downloaded the App to a mobile device.  

Customer Support.  We may collect Personal Information through your communications with our customer-support team.

When you use the Service, we and our third-party service providers, including Google Analytics, receive and record information on our server logs from your device, including your IP address, as well as from cookies and similar technologies.  Cookies are small text files placed in visitors’ computer browsers to store their preferences.  Most browsers allow you to block and delete cookies.  However, if you do that, the Service may not work properly.
